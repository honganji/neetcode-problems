# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock with Cooldown

[LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock-with-cooldown/)

Given a list of daily stock prices, find the most profit you can make by buying and selling as many times as you like, holding at most one share at a time, where after each sale you must skip one day before buying again.
