# Car Fleet

[LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/car-fleet/)

Given cars' starting positions and speeds on a one-lane road, count how many
groups (fleets) arrive at the target, where a car that catches up to a slower
car ahead merges into its fleet and travels at that slower speed.
