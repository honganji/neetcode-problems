# Pacific Atlantic Water Flow

[LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/pacific-atlantic-water-flow/)

Given a grid of cell heights, water can flow from a cell to any neighbor that is equal or lower. The Pacific touches the top and left edges and the Atlantic touches the bottom and right edges. Return every cell from which water can reach both oceans.
